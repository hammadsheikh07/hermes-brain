## Overview
Nous Research is the AI lab that builds [[hermes-agent]]. It is "the lab behind the Hermes, Nomos,
and Psyche models" and operates **Nous Portal**, a one-account gateway to 300+ models used by
Hermes (see [[model-providers]]). (source: raw/articles/2026-07-01-hermes-agent-docs.md)

## Key facts
- Creator and maintainer of Hermes Agent (open source, MIT) — (source: raw/articles/2026-07-01-hermes-agent-docs.md)
- Known for the Hermes, Nomos, and Psyche model families — (source: raw/articles/2026-07-01-hermes-agent-docs.md)
- Operates Nous Portal — "300+ models," the recommended one-account setup for Hermes — (source: raw/articles/2026-07-01-hermes-agent-github.md)

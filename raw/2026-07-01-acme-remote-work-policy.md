# Acme Co. — Remote Work Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Head of People (Operations team).

Acme is **remote-first**. Employees may work from anywhere within ±3 hours of US Central Time
so that async handoffs stay same-day.

## Core expectations

- **Async-by-default.** Decisions and updates are written down (docs or threads), not left in
  meetings. Anyone should be able to catch up by reading.
- **Anchor days.** Each team gathers in person **twice per quarter** ("anchor days"). Travel is
  reimbursed. These are for planning and relationship-building, not routine work.
- **Core overlap hours.** 10:00–14:00 US Central is the shared window for synchronous calls.
- **Response time.** Non-urgent messages: within one business day. Urgent (on-call/incident):
  per the on-call rotation.

## Equipment & stipend

- One-time home-office stipend of **$750** for new hires.
- Company laptop provided; hardware refresh every 3 years.

## Notes

The Engineering team runs an on-call rotation that overrides the standard response-time
expectation during incidents.

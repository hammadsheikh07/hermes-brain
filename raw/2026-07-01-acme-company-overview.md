# Acme Co. — Company Overview

Acme Co. is a SaaS company building analytics software for the logistics industry. Founded in
2022, we help mid-market freight and warehousing operators see their operations in real time.

- **Stage:** Series A (raised $12M in Q1 2026, led by Northwind Ventures).
- **Headcount:** ~40 people across Product, Engineering, Sales, and Operations.
- **Headquarters:** registered in Austin, TX, but the company operates **remote-first**.
- **Flagship product:** *Acme Pulse*, a dashboard that ingests carrier and warehouse events and
  surfaces delays, cost anomalies, and SLA risk.

## Teams

- **Engineering** (~14 people) builds and operates Acme Pulse. Led by the VP of Engineering.
- **Product** (~5) owns roadmap and design.
- **Sales & Success** (~12) handles mid-market accounts.
- **Operations** (~9) covers finance, people, and support.

## How we work

Acme is remote-first and async-by-default. Most coordination happens in writing; meetings are
the exception, not the default. Detailed norms live in the Remote Work Policy.

## TL;DR
- Remote-first, effective 2026-01-01, owned by the Head of People.
- Work anywhere within ±3 hours of US Central; async-by-default.
- In-person anchor days twice per quarter (travel reimbursed).
- Core overlap hours 10:00–14:00 US Central; non-urgent replies within one business day.
- $750 one-time home-office stipend; company laptop, 3-year refresh.
- Engineering on-call overrides the standard response-time rule during incidents.

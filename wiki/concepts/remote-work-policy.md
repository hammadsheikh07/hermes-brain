## Definition
[[acme-co]]'s remote-first working policy (effective 2026-01-01, owned by the Head of People).
Employees may work anywhere within ±3 hours of US Central Time; work is async-by-default with a
small shared synchronous window. (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-remote-work-policy.md)

## Details
- Location: anywhere within ±3 hours of US Central Time — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-remote-work-policy.md)
- Async-by-default: decisions and updates are written down, not left in meetings — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-remote-work-policy.md)
- Anchor days: each team meets in person **twice per quarter**; travel reimbursed — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-remote-work-policy.md)
- Core overlap hours: 10:00–14:00 US Central — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-remote-work-policy.md)
- Response time: non-urgent within one business day — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-remote-work-policy.md)
- Home-office stipend: $750 one-time for new hires; laptop provided, 3-year refresh — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-remote-work-policy.md)

## Applies to
- All of [[acme-co]]
- [[engineering-team]] — with an on-call exception to the response-time rule during incidents

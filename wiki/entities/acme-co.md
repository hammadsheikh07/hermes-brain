## Summary
Acme Co. is a Series A SaaS company (founded 2022) building analytics for the logistics
industry. Its flagship product, *Acme Pulse*, gives mid-market freight and warehousing
operators real-time visibility into delays, cost anomalies, and SLA risk. The company is
**remote-first** with ~40 people. (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-company-overview.md)

## Key facts
- Stage: Series A — raised $12M in Q1 2026, led by Northwind Ventures — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-company-overview.md)
- Headcount: ~40 across Product, Engineering, Sales & Success, and Operations — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-company-overview.md)
- HQ registered in Austin, TX; operates remote-first — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-company-overview.md)
- Flagship product: *Acme Pulse* — logistics operations dashboard — (source: raw/2026-07-01-acme-company-overview.md)
